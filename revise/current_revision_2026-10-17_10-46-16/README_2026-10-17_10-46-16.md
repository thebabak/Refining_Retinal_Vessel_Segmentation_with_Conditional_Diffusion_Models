## Existing numerical records

`records/` contains the unchanged previously calculated values used for Table 6, per-image Tables 7–9 and OOF Table 10. The selected global/frozen CSV contains the same reference configuration as Table 6, rather than the separate full-pipeline experiment. Per-image and training-seed averages, paired confidence intervals and OOF summaries were copied from their existing analysis. No analysis was rerun for this release.

Table 6's paired intervals are exploratory crossed-bootstrap intervals. The OOF intervals and Holm-corrected probabilities concern OOF minus in-sample, a different contrast. These intervals do not demonstrate equivalence or rule out training-input shift. The repeated-sampling study uses one trained checkpoint and twenty initial-noise seeds, not twenty independent trainings.

The historical raw checkpoints and probability arrays are not packaged here. Follow the manuscript's availability statement for retained artifacts; complete probability arrays for every repeat of the fixed-checkpoint sampling study were not retained. Historical result tables elsewhere in the repository must not replace these version-specific records.
