Thank you for this clarification. The input mask contains512x512=262,144 scalar values, whereas the encoded grid contains64x128x128=1,048,576, four times as many. It is downsampled fourfold along each spatial axis but does not reduce total scalar count. We have revised the manuscript to describe our encoded mask consistently as a spatially downsampled multi-channel representation, replacing feature-space and latent terminology for the proposed representation. Parameter efficiency refers to the model parameter budget rather than the number of encoded values. Canonical names of cited prior methods and publication titles are retained.
