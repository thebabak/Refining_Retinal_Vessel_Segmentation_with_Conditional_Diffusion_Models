Thank you for this comment. We performed an additional CHASE-DB1 experiment with a deterministic spatial residual U-Net that receives the RGB image and coarse vessel probabilities at full spatial resolution, without an autoencoder or diffusion sampling. The network has488,145 learned parameters and uses a BCE/Dice objective and aligned geometric augmentation. Five refiners were independently trained with validation-based checkpoint selection, including a zero-residual identity candidate. All40 per-image/seed records were verified against saved FP32 predictions and TP/FP/FN/TN.
Mean Dice was0.810514(SD0.001806) for the paired coarse predictions and0.810393(SD0.001847) for the selected refiner. Mean change was-0.000121, with an exploratory crossed-bootstrap95%CI[-0.000343,0.000011]; paired training-seed t p=0.219939 and exact signed-rank p=0.25. The identity candidate was selected in two of five runs. Thus no reliable improvement over the paired coarse baseline was established.
This is a separate experiment using retained executable coarse-predictor checkpoints; it is not a controlled comparison with the original diffusion score-producing configuration. It does not demonstrate superiority of diffusion over deterministic refinement. Same-protocol evaluation of HiDiffSeg, RefineCatDiff and Li et al. also remains outstanding. The original supplied tables are unchanged.
